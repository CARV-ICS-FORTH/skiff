# Skiff Governance

This document describes the governance model of the Skiff project.

Skiff is currently maintained by its small group of founding developers:

- Antony Chazapis (`@chazapis`_)
- Evangelos Maliaroudakis (`@malvag`_)
- Konstantinos Kampadais (`@Kampadais`_)
- Giannis Petsis (`@gpetsis`_)

As the project matures, governance may evolve to assign different roles to different people. For now, all maintainers collectively steer the project.

We welcome external contributions and will consider adding maintainers to the core team based on sustained and constructive involvement.
